## 1. Service Name

**Single-Site Preliminary Intelligence Brief (California BESS)**

## 2. What the Customer Provides

When a prospective customer submits an inquiry through the request form at `geospatialabs.com/request`, they provide:

| Input Field                | Required | Description                                                        |
| -------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Full Name                  | Yes      | Primary point of contact for the engagement.                       |
| Work Email                 | Yes      | Email used for scope confirmation and delivery.                    |
| Company Name               | Yes      | Entity requesting the screen.                                      |
| Role / Title               | Yes      | To understand decision authority and diligence use case.           |
| Candidate Site Location    | Yes      | Address, county, APN, coordinates, or geographic description.      |
| Project Type               | Yes      | Utility-scale BESS, C&I BESS, BTM, Solar+BESS, or Other.          |
| Approximate Capacity (MW)  | Optional | Target nameplate capacity.                                         |
| Approximate Duration (hrs) | Optional | Target storage duration.                                           |
| Development Stage          | Yes      | Pre-screening, site control, pre-application, or application.      |
| Primary Decision Question  | Yes      | What they are trying to decide (≥10 characters).                   |
| Additional Notes           | Optional | Any supplementary context, constraints, or clarifications.         |

## 3. Scope of the Deliverable

A Single-Site Preliminary Intelligence Brief covers **9 sections**:

1. **Candidate-Site Overview** — jurisdiction, parcel, acreage, project assumptions.
2. **Grid & Interconnection Context** — nearby substations, published queue data, ICA/RAM portals.
3. **Parcel & Jurisdiction Context** — cadastral data, zoning, overlay districts.
4. **Permitting & Planning Signals** — CUP history, CEQA filings, local ordinances.
5. **Environmental & Siting Considerations** — FHSZ, FIRM, CDFW BIOS, NWI.
6. **Relevant Nearby Project Activity** — operational comps, withdrawn projects.
7. **4-Part Evidence Taxonomy** — VERIFIED / UNKNOWN / CONFLICTING / DEEPER DILIGENCE.
8. **Identified Source References** — traceable bibliography with agency citations.
9. **Questions for Next-Stage Diligence** — targeted specialist inquiry lists.

### Evidence Classification

Every material finding is tagged:

| Status             | Meaning                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **VERIFIED**       | Documented in identified public filing with timestamp and citation.      |
| **UNKNOWN**        | Public records are silent or unindexed; explicitly labeled, not guessed. |
| **CONFLICTING**    | Two authoritative sources disagree; surfaced side-by-side.               |
| **DEEPER DILIGENCE** | Published signal indicates constraint requiring specialist inspection. |

## 4. Explicit Exclusions

The pilot brief does **not** include:

- Final engineering designs or interconnection feasibility studies.
- Utility capacity determinations or queue position guarantees.
- Legal opinions, title searches, or ALTA survey analysis.
- Environmental clearance, biological surveys, or Phase I/II ESAs.
- Financial modeling, revenue projections, or IRR calculations.
- Land acquisition negotiation, lease structuring, or brokerage.
- Payment processing — no payment is requested at the inquiry stage.

## 5. Pricing Confirmation Workflow

1. Customer submits inquiry via `/request` form.
2. GeoSpatia Labs reviews candidate location and evaluation question.
3. If the site is within California research scope, GeoSpatia Labs replies by email with:
   - Confirmed scope of the brief.
   - Fixed fee for the single-site screen (quoted per engagement).
   - Estimated delivery timeline.
4. Customer confirms scope and fee by email before any research begins.
5. **No research is initiated without explicit scope and fee confirmation.**

## 6. Delivery Format

- Delivered as a structured PDF and/or web-based report.
- Includes source bibliography with public access URLs and docket numbers.
- Shared securely to the customer's confirmed work email.

## 7. Review & Quality Process

1. Qualified analyst assembles public records from identified statutory sources.
2. Every finding is classified with the 4-part evidence taxonomy.
3. Internal QA review confirms source citations, taxonomy accuracy, and completeness.
4. Final brief is delivered only after internal sign-off.

## 8. Current Geographic Scope

- **California only** — specifically focused on candidate Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) sites.
- Other states or technologies may be evaluated on a case-by-case basis in future.
